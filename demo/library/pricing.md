# Workshop pricing 2026

- Full day, up to 12 people: £2,400
- Full day, 13 to 20 people: £3,100
- Half day, up to 12 people: £1,450
- Groups larger than 20 are split into separate sessions.
- Booking three or more sessions together: 10% off the total.
- Travel inside London is included. Travel outside London is charged at cost.
